You flow in my veins,<br>
Rustle in the air that I breathe,<br>
But not the only reason to wake up.<br>

You are a part of how I carry myself,<br>
The way I treat people,<br>
But not my only root.<br>

You are the color that paints me,<br>
The light that lights my crevices,<br>
But not the only greens of my tree.<br>

You are my deepest yearning,<br>
Your eyes, my gaze would ease into,<br>
But not my only destination.<br>
