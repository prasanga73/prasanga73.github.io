I know you want to have it all but not enough at a time,<br>
I know you want yourself but also your every fiery desire,<br>
I know you want to touch the sparks  but under your circumstances,<br>
I know you want to want me but fail me in the consequences of your matters,<br>
I know you behold your mind and heart clashing but always let mind prevail,<br>
I know you want to show love but only under your premise,<br>
I know you want to taste the extremes but limit yourself to just wanting,<br>
And,<br>
In the end,<br>
I knew everything, yet still,<br>
Things happened and roads diverged<br>
